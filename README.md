Global Superstore — Executive BI Dashboard

Business Intelligence project built for the AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship (Week 2: Business Analytics Case Study).

Business scenario

Acting as a Junior BI Analyst for AnalystLab Africa Consulting, this project designs an executive dashboard for a national retail company's management team to monitor sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and regional performance.

Dataset

Global Superstore Dataset (Kaggle) — multi-market retail transactions spanning:

Regions/Markets: Central, North, North Asia, South, Central Asia, and others
Customer segments: Consumer, Corporate, Home Office
Product categories: Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology
Business questions answered
What is the overall sales performance of the company?
Which regions generate the highest sales and profit?
Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
Which product categories perform best?
Which products are the most profitable?
What trends can be observed over time?
What recommendations should management implement to improve business performance?
Tools used
Microsoft Power BI Desktop — dashboard development
Power Query — data cleaning and transformation (missing values, duplicate removal, data type correction, calculated columns)
DAX — custom measures (e.g. Profit Margin %)
Dashboard contents
5 KPI cards: Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Average Sales, Profit Margin
2 bar charts, 2 column charts, 1 line chart, 1 donut chart, 1 map, 1 matrix table
Interactive slicers for date range and category
A dedicated Insights Summary page
Key insights
Technology is the top-performing category, with Phones as the single highest-selling sub-category.
The Central region generates the highest profit of all regions.
Overall sales and profit show steady growth from 2011 to 2014.
Furniture converts less of its sales into profit than Technology or Office Supplies, indicating a margin issue worth addressing.
Company-wide profit margin sits at approximately 14%.
